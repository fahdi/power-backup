## Authorised distributors and warranty
- Who officially distributes Victron, Deye (and any local rebrands) in this country?
- Are grey-market imports region-locked or excluded from warranty?

## Local prices
- Inverter (8 kW and 12 kW class) and battery (per module and per kWh), in local currency, with source and date.

## Grid rules
- Net metering / net billing / export limits and buyback rate.
- Connection type (single / three phase) and sanctioned-load or main-breaker limits.
- Typical outage pattern and grid voltage range.

## Climate and installation
- Peak temperatures (inverter derating, battery placement).
- Dust / humidity (IP rating needed).
- Local wiring and earthing requirements, and permits.
